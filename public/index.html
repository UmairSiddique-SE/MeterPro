Three steps

From meter to record in under a second

Built for the exact sequence a field visit actually follows.

01 — POINT

Aim at the display

Line up the digits inside the frame. Works on analog dials and digital LCD panels alike.

02 — SCAN

On-device OCR reads it

The digits are recognized instantly, on the phone — no upload needed to get a result.

03 — SYNC

Reading is saved and timestamped

Every scan is logged with the meter ID, time, and location, then pushed to the cloud once you're online.

What it handles

Everything a reading round needs

Instant OCR reading

Recognizes digit wheels and digital displays without a data connection.

Works fully offline

Basements and utility rooms rarely have signal. Scans queue and sync when you're back online.

Automatic cloud sync

Readings back up to Firebase the moment a connection is available — nothing to export manually.

History & export

Every past reading per meter, searchable, and exportable as a spreadsheet for reporting.

Multi-meter profiles

Track dozens of meters across sites, each with its own ID, unit type, and reading history.

Manual correction

Smudged glass or a worn dial won't stop you — edit any digit before it's saved, with a note attached.
