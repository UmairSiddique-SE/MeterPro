Workflow

Simple, Fast, and Accurate.

Log your meter readings in under five seconds without typing numbers or doing manual calculations.

STEP 01

Aim Your Camera

Open the MeterPro scanner and point your phone at the electricity meter display. The on-device OCR engine detects numeric digits live.

STEP 02

Verify & Confirm

Review the recognized reading with a single tap. MeterPro calculates units consumed since your previous reading instantly.

STEP 03

Track Trends & Save

View daily consumption graphs, get morning and evening reminder alerts, and predict your monthly electricity invoice ahead of time.

All Features

Engineered for Complete Utility Control

Everything you need to avoid surprise bills, track high consumption appliances, and stay on top of meter units.

📸

Camera OCR Scanner

State-of-the-art text recognition reads both digital LCD and mechanical rotating meter dials directly through your camera.

⚡

Accurate Bill Calculator

Pre-configured with standard tariff slabs, taxes, fuel price adjustment (FPA), and electricity duty for pinpoint forecasts.

⏰

Smart Reminders

Automated daily notifications remind you to record your reading at your chosen time (Morning, Evening, or Night).

📊

Usage Trends & Graphs

Interactive bar and line charts illustrate daily spikes, weekly averages, and month-over-month comparisons.

🏢

Multi-Meter Management

Track separate meters for your upper portion, basement, shop, rental property, or solar net-metering in one single app.

☁️

Cloud Sync & Backup

Firebase authentication and cloud database backup keep your historical readings safe even if you switch devices.
